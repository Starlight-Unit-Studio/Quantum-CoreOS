# Quantum CoreOS Branding and Trademark Policy

## Protected project identity

The following names and visual elements identify the Starlight Unit Studios project:

- `Quantum CoreOS`
- `Quantum CoreOS by Starlight Unit Studios`
- the fictional in-universe designation `Quantum Core OS`
- `Starlight Unit Studios`
- the Ren Matrix project asset
- the official Quantum CoreOS interface identity, logos, word marks, and origin notices

No trademark registration is asserted by this file unless separately documented. Copyright, naming, unfair-competition, and other applicable rights remain reserved.

## Required presentation

The real product name is **Quantum CoreOS**. A prominent first reference should preferably use `Quantum CoreOS by Starlight Unit Studios`.

Built-in names, copyright notices, About information, source attribution, and official origin notices must remain visible and must not be removed, replaced, covered, or presented in a misleadingly minor role.

Additional themes and operator identities may be added. They must not erase the official origin of the project or present an unofficial build as an official Starlight Unit Studios release.

A publicly distributed modified version must state:

`This is an unofficial modification of Quantum CoreOS and is not maintained or officially supported by Starlight Unit Studios.`

## Fictional references

`Quantum Core OS v2163.4`, `Quantum-UNIX`, Tri-Solaris Alliance, Hyperion Node, TCI, and related terms may appear as creative in-universe references. Their use in the project does not claim current real-world certification, hardware, affiliation, or technical capability.

## Technical identifiers

Compatibility identifiers such as `quantum-coreos`, `QCORE_*`, service names, package names, API paths, repository slugs, and domain components may remain unchanged. They are machine-facing identifiers and do not authorize another product to claim official Starlight Unit Studios status.

## Third-party names

Names such as Linux, UNIX, Debian, systemd, KDE, COSMIC, Ollama, Gemma, and other third-party products are used only to describe ancestry, compatibility, dependencies, or planned integration. Their marks and names remain subject to the rights of their respective owners.

Quantum CoreOS does not claim UNIX certification. `Quantum-UNIX` is a fictional designation within the Starlight Unit setting.

## Permissions

Any branding change outside the terms of the Quantum CoreOS Community Source License requires prior express written permission from the rights holder. Permission for one distribution does not create a general permission for another distribution.
