# Starlight Unit Studios Quantum Community Source License Family

Status: version 1.0 decision

The Quantum projects use a shared Community Source philosophy with separate project-specific license texts.

The German `LICENSE.de.md` file in each repository is legally controlling. The English `LICENSE.md` file is a convenience translation.

All three licenses are custom Source Available licenses and are not OSI-approved open-source licenses.

## Shared principles

- royalty-free private and internal use
- internal commercial use
- no user-count or license-enforcement telemetry requirement
- source inspection and modification
- distribution of modified versions only with attribution, corresponding source, the same license, and clear unofficial-modification labeling
- protected product names and Starlight Unit Studios origin notices
- no sale or white-label resale of the Quantum products themselves
- third-party components remain under their own licenses
- written case-specific permissions remain possible

## Quantum Runtime

Quantum Runtime may be used as an internal component of separate commercial applications and services.

It may not be sold, white-labeled, or offered as a paid standalone general-purpose Runtime or inference service without prior written permission.

## Quantum Control

Quantum Control may be used for paid hosting and managed-service operation. Customers may receive authenticated access limited to resources provided or managed for them.

Quantum Control itself may not be sold, sublicensed, white-labeled, or offered as a standalone paid control-panel, SaaS, or general Control API product without prior written permission.

## Quantum CoreOS

Quantum CoreOS may be used to operate commercial services. Hardware, installation, configuration, support, compute, storage, network, and management charges are permitted.

Quantum CoreOS itself may not be sold as a software license, activation, paid download, image subscription, standalone operating system, or white-label distribution without prior written permission.

Systems may be transferred with Quantum CoreOS preinstalled when no separate CoreOS license fee is charged and all distribution, source, notice, and branding conditions are satisfied.

## Ember CoreUI relationship

The Quantum licenses are based on the established Ember CoreUI Community Source approach but are separate licenses. Ember CoreUI keeps its own license, repository, product identity, installer, versioning, and standalone Repack lifecycle.

## Legal review status

The license family is a deliberate project policy and release gate. Because it is a custom license, professional legal review remains advisable before broad commercial reliance or the first major public binary distribution.
